A portion of my dissertation project required a supplementary experiment which explored the actual physical changes to the larynx undergoing a structural displacement. While the participant vocalized the vowel /i/, researcher performed a laryngoscopy of the participant and recorded 30fps video of the participant's larynx as it was displaced using our displacement device. For full experimental details please see [Smith et al. 2020](https://djsmith17.github.io/publication/2020-06-01-Smith2020). 

In pursuit of defining the extent that the larynx moved to this type of stimulus, I developed a GUI to more easily work with and create manual measures. Below is an example of what the GUI looks like before any video is loaded in.
